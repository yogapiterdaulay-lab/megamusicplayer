Privacy Policy for Mega Music Player

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SZ Studio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with your use of our mobile application, Mega Music Player (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data interactions.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to Mega Music Player. Our application is designed to function primarily as a local, offline audio playback tool. By downloading, installing, or using Mega Music Player, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Because Mega Music Player operates locally on your device, we do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal or sensitive user data.

  • Personal Information: We do not require account creation, registration, login credentials, or the submission of personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.
  • Audio Files and Local Storage: The App accesses your device's local storage solely to read, index, and play audio files that you explicitly select or store on your device. This media content remains entirely on your local hardware and is never uploaded to any external servers or cloud databases controlled by us.
  • Analytics and Telemetry: We do not embed third-party tracking frameworks, advertising SDKs, or analytics tools that profile your behavior or track your usage habits.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

To deliver core playback functionality, the App may request specific Android system permissions. These permissions are handled locally on your device:

  • Storage / Media Access: Used strictly to scan local directories and play your local music files.
  • Audio Focus / Foreground Services: Used to manage playback states (such as pausing music when receiving a phone call) and to run audio controls smoothly in the notification shade.

We do not use these permissions to harvest contacts, location data, camera feeds, or identifying device info.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Mega Music Player does not integrate third-party analytics, social trackers, or ad networks. If our store listing or promotional channels contain links to external sites, those external sites operate under their own independent privacy policies, for which we assume no responsibility.

5. Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit your personal data over the internet, your data remains secure on your personal device. Local files are protected by your device's built-in operating system security architecture.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect any data from any user regardless of age, it is inherently safe for all age groups.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates to the App or changes in regulatory standards. Any modifications will be posted directly on this publicly accessible page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
